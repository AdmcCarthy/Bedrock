# Data licence

The licence in `LICENSE` (MIT License with Acknowledgement) covers the code and the pages. The layers under `site/data/` are derived, generalised displays of the sources below and keep their terms. The site is non-commercial and attributes every source on its pages; the pipelines that made the layers are private and the raw maps are not copied here.

## Geological maps shown

Only maps whose publishers allow open display are published. A unit's polygon here is a simplified, merged version of the survey's map at a stated level of detail; its attributes are the survey's words read into one vocabulary.

- Norway, NGU bedrock 1:1 350 000: Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data (NLOD 2.0). "Contains data made available by NGU under NLOD."
- Sweden, SGU bedrock 1:1 000 000: SGU open geodata, free to use and publish. "Source: SGU."
- Finland, GTK generalised bedrock 1:1 000 000: GTK Open Licence, compatible with CC BY 4.0. "Contains data from the Geological Survey of Finland (GTK)."
- Great Britain, BGS Geology 625k (DiGMapGB-625 v5): Open Government Licence as reported by BGS. "Contains NERC materials © NERC."
- Northern Ireland, GSNI bedrock 1:250 000: Open Government Licence v3.0. "Contains GSNI data under the Open Government Licence v3.0."
- Ireland, GSI bedrock 1:500 000: CC BY 4.0 (read from the licence file in the archive). "Contains Irish Public Sector Data (Geological Survey Ireland) licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence."
- Austria, GeoSphere geological units 1:500 000: CC BY 4.0 for the base map, as reported. "GeoSphere Austria."

Not published here because the terms are unverified or restrict display: the IGME 5000 (BGR, free with citation but no licence named), Poland's PIG maps (no terms found), Spain's IGME 1:1M (IGME reuse conditions), Greenland's GEUS 1:500 000 (no licence set). The private pipelines hold them; they can be added when their terms are read.

## Catchments

HydroBASINS and HydroRIVERS v1 (HydroSHEDS): free for scientific, educational and commercial use with attribution per the product pages; some catalogues record CC BY 4.0, an older record CC BY-NC 3.0. "Lehner, B., Grill G. (2013): Global river hydrography and network routing: baseline data and new approaches to study the world's large river systems. Hydrological Processes 27(15). Data from HydroSHEDS, https://www.hydrosheds.org."

The composition statistics attached to each catchment were computed from the maps above and, where no open national map covers the ground (Denmark, Germany, the Baltic states, Poland on the North-east Europe frame), from the IGME 5000 (BGR; "Asch, K. and the IGME 5000 project, DOI 10.25928/igme-5000") and Poland's PIG 1:1M map. Those are statistics, not map polygons; the maps themselves are not shown.

## Terrain

Hillshade and land bands are Relief products (github.com/AdmcCarthy/Relief) from Copernicus DEM GLO-90 (© DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus 2014–2018, provided under Copernicus by the European Union and ESA), EMODnet Bathymetry (EMODnet Bathymetry Consortium 2024), NOAA ETOPO 2022, and on the Norwegian Sea frame Kartverket and MAREANO under the Norwegian licence for open data. Climate zones used in the budget: Beck and others (2023), Köppen-Geiger 1991–2020, 1 km, CC BY 4.0.

## Chart colours

Age colours follow the International Chronostratigraphic Chart (ICS, CGMW colours), cited as "International Chronostratigraphic Chart, ICS/CGMW". The vintage and paper palettes are this project's own.
